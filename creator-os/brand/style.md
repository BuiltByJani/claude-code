## Voice (captions, scripts, on-screen text)
_Learned from all 114 captions in `brand/voice-samples/captions.txt` (Oct 27 – Nov 25, 2026)._

**The core finding:** Jani's best posts sound like she's talking through tears or fire, unedited.
The polished, templated captions from late November ("I'm Jani. 34, starting over, building my
brand with a phone and free AI…") read as AI-written, and they are her worst performers
(49–2,379 views, mostly under 800). Write like the top posts, never like those.

- **Who she talks to:** women starting over. "Ladies", "girl", "sis", "Girls…". She talks *with*
  them, not down to them: "We're figuring this out together, one day at a time. 🦋"
- **Sentence shape:** short punches, then a long, breathless run-on when the story gets real.
  Ellipses everywhere for pauses: "I don't cry for me… I'm not a victim." Line breaks between
  thoughts. Lists of "Hard was…" lines build pressure.
- **CAPS for the emotional peak**, not for decoration: "IVE SUFFERED ENOUGH! IT WAS MY TURN!",
  "JESUS THANK YOU FOR TODAY".
- **Faith is natural, not a tagline:** "But with God, all things would work out. 🫶🏻🤲🏻",
  "Jesus I love you. 🙏🏻", "Thank you Jesus…". Use it where she would, not on every post.
- **Raw honesty, including present-tense struggle:** "I'm still healing. I'm still figuring shit
  out.", "And to be honest I've still been waking up late & miserable". She never pretends to
  have it all figured out.
- **Swearing:** yes, often censored: "f*ck", "F*ck him. Ima be rich, you watch!", "get the f*ck up!",
  "Just get the shit fking done!". Defiance toward doubters is part of the voice.
- **Her phrases (reuse them, they're hers):** "Nothing changes if nothing changes", "1% better
  everyday", "BECOME / becoming her", "play your cards or they will play you", "the word NO only
  exists if you allow it", "Not a victim", "turning my mess into a message", "Watch me.",
  "You got this 💪🏻", "PS." endings ("PS. YOU ARE WORTHY", "PS. The judges who said I couldn't are
  up my as*").
- **Humour and self-awareness:** "Ps. i just realized I should've cut the clip w second ahead 🤦‍♀️ lol",
  the 90s-love caption ("Gen Z… comments are open, but enter at your own risk. 😂😂").
- **Emoji:** 🫶🏻 🙏🏻 🤲🏻 💞 ✝️ 🦋 💪🏻 😭 😂 🫂. 1–4 per caption, at line ends, never as bullets.
- **Hashtags:** 2–5 at the end after ". . ." spacer lines: #becomingher #womeninspiringwomen
  #personalgrowth #builtbyjani. Never a wall of tags.
- **Never use:** "I'm Jani." as the first line; tidy brand-statement openers ("I rebuilt my worth
  before I rebuilt my money, and…"); numerals in place of words for style ("1 phone", "1 kept
  promise"); corporate words (asset, leverage, strategy, unlock, empire talk without a story);
  fear-selling ("your job will be replaced by a robot", 108–724 views).
- **How to open (from what actually worked):** the raw line itself: "I don't cry for me… I'm not a
  victim." (7,951), "The ladies say I need to… 😬" (6,533), a date that hurts: "November 15th will
  be the 3 year anniversary…" (5,336), a callout: "Sir, I already saved myself. 😌" (4,176), "I used
  to ask God, 'Why?' 😢" (4,363).
- **How to end:** one human line ("Send this to a woman who saved herself."), or faith, or "Watch
  me." If there's an offer, it comes after the story and stays short: "Comment BLUEPRINT…" /
  "Comment WORTH…". Story-first posts with a soft CTA beat CTA-first posts every time in her data.
- **Credibility line:** allowed without her name, after the story starts, never first.
- **Typos:** keep her cadence and slang ("Ima", "lol", "na"), but Quality fixes real typos
  ("Common blueprint" → "Comment", "nee things" → "new things") before posting.

## Formats
- Reels: 1080×1920, 7–30 s unless told otherwise
- YouTube: 1920×1080 + 1280×720 thumbnail
- Carousel: 1080×1350, 8 slides default
- Stories: 1080×1920

## Never on Instagram (Nov 27)
- jail, prison, incarcerated, locked up, addiction, addict, relapse, drugs, rehab. See PLAYBOOK 5c for approved swaps and the story vault.

## Do
- Lead with the emotional truth in the first line and the first 1–2 seconds of video.
- Story first, lesson second, offer last (if at all).
- One new video per idea. Each post should have a reason to save or share.

## Don't
- Don't open with her name or a bio line.
- Don't repost the same video several times. In the data, every repost of an identical caption
  dropped to 100–700 views (e.g. "I spent my entire twenties…" posted 3×: 3,038 / 667 / 122).
- Don't post more than 2–3 times a day. Quality over volume.
