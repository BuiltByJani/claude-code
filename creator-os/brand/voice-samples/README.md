# Voice samples

Drop the creator's real material here before the first job. Without it, nothing can be
written "in their voice" — it would be a guess.

- `captions.txt` — 20–50 past captions, one per paragraph, best performers first
- `scripts/` — transcripts or scripts of videos they recorded (or the videos themselves in
  `inbox/`; Editing's `transcribe.py` will produce transcripts)
- `top-posts.md` — links to their 10 best posts and, if known, why they worked

Then run `/learn-voice` and the Brand department fills the Voice section of `brand/style.md`.
